# AI Transition 2026

A public learning log documenting my transition from laboratory operations towards AI-assisted automation and digital transformation.

**Status: early stage. Started August 2026. No project in this repository has been built, tested or deployed yet.**

## About me

I am Giuseppe Russo, a Senior Lab Technical Administrator at the Cambridge Graphene Centre, University of Cambridge, based in Cambridge, UK. I have more than 10 years of experience in laboratory operations, equipment management, safety and compliance (SOPs, risk assessments, COSHH), procurement and user support. I do not carry out experimental research as my main activity.

My technical level is beginner. I use AI tools to help develop solutions, and I focus on understanding the logic well enough to inspect, test and validate the results.

## Goals

- Learn generative AI and prompt engineering.
- Learn Microsoft Power Automate and Power BI for workflow automation and reporting.
- Build small, practical solutions for real laboratory operations problems, starting with a Minimum Viable Product (MVP).
- Develop a portfolio of documented projects, with evidence of testing and outcomes.
- Work towards recognised Microsoft AI and data certifications (planned, not yet obtained).

## Principles

- Start with an MVP and solve a real problem before adding complexity.
- Keep human review and approval for safety, compliance and quality decisions. AI does not replace formal sign-off.
- Use only synthetic or example data in this repository. No real laboratory, personal or confidential data will be published.
- Distinguish clearly between what is planned, implemented, tested and validated.
- Do not claim benefits until they have been measured.
